# Step 4.13: Run the screening probe

Status: complete; independently verified ([review](../reviews/step-4.13-independent-review.md)).
Recorded November 18, 2026. Summary: [step-4.13-screening-probe.json](../results/step-4.13-screening-probe.json).

## What happened

We took one matched pair, the model-seed-1 control and λ = 0.3 (an
accuracy-failed diagnostic), and checked whether its **16.0%** reduction in
logical gate changes survives on real iCE40 cells. On the mapped netlist, core
toggles fell by only **3.2%** (M1), or **3.9%** fanout-weighted (M2).

Both are above the frozen 2% line, so the rule says **CONTINUE**. The margin
is thin, and most of the logical saving disappears during technology mapping.

| Seed 1 | Control | λ = 0.3 | Reduction |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Logical gate changes (step 4.5) | 379,602 | 318,730 | 16.0% |
| M1 mapped core-net toggles | 384,982 | 372,638 | **3.2%** |
| M2 fanout-weighted | 1,061,277 | 1,019,592 | **3.9%** |
| LUT4 cells | 2,908 | 2,829 | 79 fewer |

## Why a probe first

The full hardware pilot (steps 3.11 and 4.14) takes days. If the reduction
had already collapsed below 2% on mapped cells, routing could not rescue it,
and we would have paused. The frozen rule from
[step 4.12](4.12-freeze-the-hardware-stop-rule.md) fixed that decision before
we saw any number.

## How

`scripts/probe_mapped_switching.py` does the following for each model:

1. Checks the model's training manifest and protocol (seed 1, λ, deferred
   test), and that its folder and hash match the frozen policy list.
2. Exports the head-only core with the 270 **validation** images in saved
   order. Test data are never loaded.
3. Simulates the RTL: 270/270 scores and classes exact.
4. Synthesizes for iCE40 UltraPlus with Yosys, then simulates the mapped
   netlist: 270/270 exact, zero unknown values.
5. Counts toggles on core nets (LUT4, carry and flip-flop outputs) in the
   window (first drive − half period − 1 ps, last output check]. M2 multiplies
   each net's toggles by its number of cell-input loads.

```bash
.venv/bin/python scripts/probe_mapped_switching.py \
  runs/step4.5-seeds.vyq2h2nf/seed_1_control runs/step4.5-seeds.vyq2h2nf/seed_1_penalty \
  --policy docs/experiments/hardware-switching-stop-rule-v3.json
```

The run takes about 25 seconds and is deterministic: an independent rerun
reproduced every file hash.

## Where the 16% went

This is the verifier's analysis, with heuristic net labels; it is not a
separately validated measurement.

**1. The penalty saves in the wrong layers.** Logical changes fell 27.6%,
24.8%, 13.3% and **3.6%** in layers 1–4. Layer 4 feeds the popcount, so its
changes drive most of the downstream adder activity.

**2. Popcount and adders dominate the hardware and barely change.** LUTs
that are not a single gate (popcount adders and merged gate chains) carry
about 60% of mapped toggles. They changed by only 0.3%.

**3. Most learned gates are not physical wires.** Synthesis packs several
gates into one 4-input LUT. Only about **870 of 4,000** gates survive as their
own nets, and those fell 12.4%.

> Example: the penalty taught gate `l2_c[17]` to toggle less. On the chip,
> no wire is called `l2_c[17]`; its logic sits inside a LUT together with its
> neighbours, and that LUT's output follows the combined function.

**4. Half of the M2 gain is a wiring difference, not switching.** The input
register toggles identically in both models (13,097). In the penalized
model it drives fewer LUT inputs (2,192 vs 2,383). Without it, R2 is 2.37%.

## What this means

- The rule says CONTINUE, so steps 3.11 and 4.14 go ahead.
- A uniform gate-level penalty, mean of 2p(1−p) over all gates, does not
  target what costs switching after mapping. That cost sits in layer 4 and
  the popcount. This is useful for a characterization paper and suggests a
  better-targeted method, for example weighting later layers. That is an
  idea, not a tested result.

## Limitations

- Zero-delay mapped standalone core: no routing, glitches, power or energy.
- One seed. The 1.21-percentage-point margin over 2% may not survive routing,
  glitches or placement noise in 4.14.
- R includes shared logic (input register, popcount, registers, argmax), as
  the frozen core definition requires.
- Synthesis counts only; fit and timing are not established here.

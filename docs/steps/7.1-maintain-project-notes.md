# Step 7.1: Maintain project and learning notes

Status: completed. Notes recorded November 13, 2026.

## Why we needed this

Results and decisions should remain understandable after the conversation is forgotten.

## What we did

We kept a project context, a numbered checklist, a UART reference and these short step-by-step learning notes.

## How it works

Use PROJECT_CHECKLIST.md to see status, PROJECT_CONTEXT.md for background and saved results, and this folder's README.md to revisit one concept at a time. Each completed step should have a note linking its code, evidence and prerequisites.

## Small example

The UART protocol details stay in UART_INTERFACE.md; step 2.6 links to that older document rather than copying every byte-level detail.

## Result and its limits

Markdown is the current project record. The older Excel tracker is a separate snapshot and does not synchronize automatically. Raw generated results remain local until the archival step is completed.
